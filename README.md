# AI Customer Churn & Revenue Risk Prediction Engine

An end-to-end machine learning pipeline built in Python using Scikit-Learn to forecast SaaS subscriber attrition, isolate critical churn drivers, and quantify monthly recurring revenue (MRR) at risk.

---

## 💼 Business Problem
Customer acquisition costs in SaaS significantly outweigh retention costs. Identifying attrition risk before cancellation enables commercial and customer success teams to execute targeted retention workflows and safeguard cash flows.

---

## 🔑 Key Deliverables & Outcomes
- **Predictive Machine Learning:** Trained a supervised Random Forest classification model evaluating non-linear subscriber patterns.
- **Performance Evaluation:** Validated discriminatory performance utilizing ROC-AUC and precision/recall metrics.
- **Feature Importance Analysis:** Identified customer support ticket velocity and contract commitment terms as primary predictors of customer departure.
- **Revenue at Risk Modeling:** Mapped individual subscription probability scores directly to monthly gross contract values to quantify enterprise revenue exposure.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Skills
- **Language & Libraries:** Python, Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-Learn, Matplotlib
- **Methodology:** Data generation & feature preprocessing, train/test split validation, ensemble classification, ROC-AUC evaluation, commercial risk quantification
